## Thesis
Scientific theories are meaningful only insofar as they are *falsifiable*. Verification is easy and cheap; falsification is the honest test. A theory that cannot in principle be refuted is not scientific.

## Key claims (from context)
- Demarcation: falsifiability separates science from pseudo-science.
- Confirmation bias is intrinsic to human reasoning; the discipline of falsification counteracts it.
- Bold conjectures + rigorous attempts to refute them = scientific progress.

## Relevance to defi-investor
Foundational for the pre-registration architecture (Decision 4). Popper's falsifiability principle is the philosophical basis for the D1 red-team section ("how could this look real even if it isn't?"). Also the epistemological grounding for CHARTER §5 kill criteria — a hypothesis that can't fail is a hypothesis that can't teach.
