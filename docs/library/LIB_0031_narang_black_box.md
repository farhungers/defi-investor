## Thesis
Accessible overview of how systematic (quantitative) investment strategies are built, structured, and operated. Wiley Finance series, third edition. Covers alpha models, risk models, transaction cost models, portfolio construction, execution.

## Key claims (from context/reputation)
- Systematic investing decomposes into: alpha model, risk model, transaction cost model, portfolio construction, execution.
- Each component can be studied and improved independently.
- "Black box" is transparent when you understand the components.
