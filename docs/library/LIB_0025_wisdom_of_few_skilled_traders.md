## Thesis
Counter to strict "wisdom of the crowd," prediction market accuracy is disproportionately driven by a small skilled minority. Paper likely shows that identifying and weighting these traders improves aggregate market accuracy.

## Relevance to defi-investor
- **Directly relevant to the C1 frame.** If a small minority of skilled traders drives price accuracy in prediction markets, the same likely holds in CEX Earn markets. Identifying that minority is the alpha.
