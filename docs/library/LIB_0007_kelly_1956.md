## Thesis
Original derivation of the Kelly criterion. Frames optimal betting fraction through Shannon information theory: bet the fraction that maximizes the expected logarithm of wealth, equivalent to maximizing the exponential growth rate of capital.

## Key claims (from context)
- Formula: f* = (bp - q) / b for a binary bet.
- Maximizes long-run compound growth of capital.
- Overbetting leads to ruin; underbetting leaves growth on the table.
