## Thesis
Compilation of mental models drawn from psychology, biology, physics, and mathematics, organized around Charlie Munger's "latticework of models" approach to decision-making.

## Key claims (from reputation)
- Multi-disciplinary models beat single-discipline reasoning.
- Checklists reduce error rates in complex domains.
- Understanding why smart people fail is as valuable as understanding why they succeed.

## Relevance to defi-investor
Meta-discipline. The pre-registration red-team requirement (Decision 4 D1) is essentially a Mungerian checklist forcing consideration of failure modes.
