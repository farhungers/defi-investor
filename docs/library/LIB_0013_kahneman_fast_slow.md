## Thesis
Human cognition operates in two systems: fast intuitive (System 1) and slow deliberate (System 2). Most cognitive errors and biases arise from System 1 overreach into decisions that require System 2.

## Key claims (from reputation)
- Anchoring, availability, representativeness heuristics.
- Loss aversion, framing effects, prospect theory.
- Substitution: hard questions replaced by easy ones without awareness.
- Regression to the mean regularly misinterpreted as signal.

## Relevance to defi-investor
Background. Two direct applications:
1. Second Law is a System-2 discipline imposed against System-1's urge to "just try one more feature" after seeing a preliminary result.
2. Kill counter (Decision 4 D3) counteracts the availability heuristic — we can't underweight prior failed hypotheses just because they're not top-of-mind.
