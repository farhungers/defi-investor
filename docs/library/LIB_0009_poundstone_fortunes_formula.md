## Thesis
Popular history of the Kelly criterion — from Bell Labs (Shannon, Kelly) to card counting (Thorp) to Wall Street. Argues Kelly is the correct betting rule for wealth-maximizing agents facing repeated favorable bets.

## Key claims (from title/context)
- Historical narrative of Kelly's adoption in gambling then finance.
- Advocates for growth-optimal (logarithmic-utility) betting.
- Critiques mean-variance orthodoxy.
