## Thesis
Mathematical treatment of position sizing. Vince's "optimal f" is a Kelly-adjacent framework that accounts for real-world trade sequences and drawdown constraints; the Leverage Space Model extends to portfolios.

## Key claims (from title/context)
- Optimal f = fraction of capital per trade that maximizes geometric growth in the observed trade sequence.
- Drawdown constraints often force sub-optimal-f sizing.
- Portfolio version accounts for correlation.

## Relevance to defi-investor
Post-gate sizing. Combined with Kelly (LIB_0007), gives the mathematical scaffolding for position sizing on a real signal.

## Open questions
- How does optimal-f behave under crypto's fat-tailed return distributions vs Kelly's log-utility assumption?
- Does the Leverage Space Model handle a single-signal, single-asset case sensibly?
