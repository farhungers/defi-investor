## Thesis
Practitioner-level overview of the Kelly Criterion, its mathematical foundation, gambling and investing applications, and the case for fractional Kelly as a lower-variance alternative.

## Key claims (from skim)
- Kelly formula f* = (bp - q) / b as decision aid; positive f* means favorable bet.
- Investing extension: considers full return distribution, not just mean/variance.
- Fractional Kelly reduces volatility drag; often preferable to full Kelly under uncertainty about true edge.
- Limitations: sensitive to probability estimation error; assumes constant odds.
